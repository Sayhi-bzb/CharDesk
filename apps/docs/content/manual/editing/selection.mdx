title: Selection

## Pan the view

Select Hand and drag an empty area. Content stays in place while your view moves. Use the minimap or [search](/editing/search/) to find distant content.

In the terminal, Option/Alt+arrow keys pan ten cells at a time. Your terminal app may intercept these shortcuts; use Hand when they are unavailable.

## Select content

In Web Canvas, switch to the selection tool. In the terminal, switch to Edit. Drag to create a rectangular range, including any empty cells within it.

Shift+arrow keys extend the range. Modified mouse clicks depend on whether the terminal forwards them, so Shift+click is not the only way to select.

Both cells of a wide CJK character or emoji belong to the same character. Copying and moving operate on complete characters.

## Move, copy, and delete

Drag an existing selection to move its content. Copy and paste at a new position to keep the original; cut removes it from the original position.

Web Canvas normally uses Command+C/X/V on macOS and Ctrl+C/X/V on other platforms. In the terminal, follow the current hints and help panel. Command shortcuts require terminal forwarding; Warp provides Ctrl+Y as a copy fallback.

Press Delete to clear selected content. Undo an accidental deletion or move before continuing. For keys that do not reach the canvas, see [Terminal shortcuts do not respond](/troubleshooting/#terminal-shortcuts-do-not-respond).
