---
title: Colors and styles
description: Apply colors and text styles to selected content.
---

## Select a target

Create a range, then open the style tools: Inspector in Web Canvas, or Style in the terminal footer dock. The selection determines what changes. Panning does not change text colors.

## Use color presets

Choose a foreground or background preset for quick styling. Use one color for headings and another for actions instead of assigning a different color to every line.

Combine colors with bold, italic, underline, and other text styles. Terminal appearance also depends on the terminal app and font.

## Switch light and dark themes

Themes change the reading environment and interface appearance. They do not recolor existing content. Explicit colors remain as written; text that works on a light background may need adjustment on a dark one.

Before sharing or exporting, check text contrast. Keep text labels for essential meaning rather than relying only on color.
