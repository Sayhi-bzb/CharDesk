## Open search

Use the search control, Command+F on macOS, or Ctrl+F on other platforms. If your terminal does not forward the shortcut, use F8.

Enter distinctive text and move between matches. Search locates specific content; the minimap shows its overall distribution.

## Check unexpected results

Shorten the query and confirm the text is in the current canvas or page. The canvas stores actual characters: similar symbols, spaces, and line breaks can affect matches.

An Agent can also find text and read the surrounding region. See [Agent workflow](/agents/workflow/).
