## Open a canvas

Open [CharDesk Canvas](https://canvas.chardesk.com/) in your browser. Explore the welcome canvas, then create your own from the session menu.

Content sits on a character grid. Place related content vertically and comparisons side by side. Panning changes your view without moving the text.

## Add your first content

1. Select the text tool, click an empty spot, and type a title.
2. Add text below it, or click to the right to start a comparison.
3. Switch to the selection tool, drag out a range, and try moving, copying, or recoloring it.

Start with a short passage before pasting longer content. Text, CJK characters, emoji, and character art can share one canvas.

## Choose where to work

- Edit, share, and collaborate in your browser: use Web Canvas.
- Work in a terminal: see [Terminal canvas](/terminal/).
- Ask an Agent to create or edit content: see [Connect an Agent](/agents/connect/).

Web and terminal canvases use different storage and controls. They do not automatically share a session. See [Sessions](/files/sessions/) for saving and recovery.
